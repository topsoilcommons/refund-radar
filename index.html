    Statuses reflect the original reports and may have changed.

    Count reports. Keep the denominator.

    Two of the three reports in this demo point to unclear swap or refund status. The third describes two missing swaps in one report. The app counts each report once, so the result is 2 of 3 reports. Estimating how common the problem is across NEAR would take a larger, representative sample.

    Give the customer a usable update.

    Put the latest known status, its timestamp, and any refund transaction link on one screen. If the evidence is missing, say the refund is unconfirmed and show where to get help. A support team could measure repeat status requests per 100 delayed swaps before and after the change.

    What the AI actually sees

    For these public reports, Refund Radar sends short summaries and internal references to NEAR AI Cloud’s confidential model. The app groups the reports and links its findings back to the sources. It uses the reports as evidence of what people experienced at the time. Checking a swap today would require current transaction records.
